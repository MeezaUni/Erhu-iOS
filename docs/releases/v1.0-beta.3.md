Erhu's third sideloading beta for iPhone. Install it over Beta 2 to keep your library, settings,
favourites and listening history. Beta 2 offers this update when the app opens, or from **Settings → About**.

### Choose a build

- **Static:** the player, VYBE, library tools, and still artwork.
- **Animated:** the same shared features plus motion-cover lookup, downloads, and playback.

Both builds require **iOS 17+**. System animated artwork requires the animated build and
**iOS 26+**; iOS controls when it is displayed. Online lyrics, artwork, and metadata features
depend on their external providers. VYBE searches library metadata and saved lyrics on-device.

### Install

1. Download the `.ipa` asset for your chosen variant below.
2. Open it in [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/) or [AltStore Classic](https://altstore.io/)
   and follow that installer's signing instructions. The IPAs are not signed for any developer
   account; your installer signs them with your Apple ID.
3. Import music with **+**, the Files app, or **Wi-Fi Transfer**.

Free Apple ID installations normally need refreshing within seven days and are subject to
Apple's active-app limit. The new Home Screen widget is an app extension, so installing this
beta uses one more of the App IDs a free Apple ID can register each week. Use the same signing
identity and app identifier when updating; keep a backup of your original music files.

### What's new since Beta 2

**Home Screen widgets**
- A **Now Playing** widget in two sizes: a wide, short one with the cover, the song, a live
  progress bar and previous, play / pause and next; and a large one that adds a bigger cover,
  the album and the next songs in the queue.
- The buttons control erhu even when it is in the background. Add the widget from the Home
  Screen's **Edit → Add Widget**. It needs the App Group your installer creates when it signs
  erhu; if the widget asks you to reinstall, install the IPA again.

**Delete from device**
- In a song's **…** menu, its long-press menu or the Now Playing **…** menu, choose
  **Delete from device** and confirm. The file is permanently deleted from the Erhu folder in
  Files, and the song is removed from the library, your playlists and the queue.

**26 languages**
- erhu is now in English, Arabic, Bengali, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Dutch, Filipino,
  French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese
  (Brazil), Russian, Spanish, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu and Vietnamese.
  iOS uses your iPhone's language, and you can pick one for erhu alone in iPhone Settings.
- Status and progress messages, tag-repair details, audio details, lyrics states, mix
  descriptions, the introduction and Wi-Fi Transfer are translated too, with real plural forms
  and durations in your language. Song, album and artist names are shown as they are.
- Translations are machine-made; corrections are welcome through the issue forms.

**Right-to-left languages** (Arabic, Persian, Urdu)
- The layout mirrors, while playback controls, the seek bar and the track carousel keep their
  left-to-right order as on iOS, so a swipe moves the cards the way your finger goes.
- Lyrics are aligned by their own script, whatever the app's language: Arabic, Hebrew, Persian
  and Urdu lyrics on the right, other lyrics on the left.
- The side menu, the tab bar and song-row swipes follow your finger; smart playlist banners keep
  their art clear of the text; Play and Shuffle keep the same size.
