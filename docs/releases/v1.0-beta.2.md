Erhu's second sideloading beta for iPhone. Install it over Beta 1 to keep your library, settings,
favourites and listening history. Beta 1 offers this update when the app opens, or from **Settings → About**.

### Choose a build

- **Static:** the player, VYBE, library tools, and still artwork.
- **Animated:** the same shared features plus motion-cover lookup, downloads, and playback.

Both builds require **iOS 17+**. System animated artwork requires the animated build and
**iOS 26+**; iOS controls when it is displayed. Online lyrics, artwork, and metadata features
depend on their external providers. VYBE searches library metadata and saved lyrics on-device.

### Install

1. Download the `.ipa` asset for your chosen variant below.
2. Open it in [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/) or [AltStore Classic](https://altstore.io/)
   and follow that installer's signing instructions. These IPAs are unsigned.
3. Import music with **+**, the Files app, or **Wi-Fi Transfer**.

Free Apple ID installations normally need refreshing within seven days and are subject to
Apple's active-app limit. Use the same signing identity and app identifier when updating;
keep a backup of your original music files.

### What's new since Beta 1

**Home and discovery**
- Separate Featured Radio Stations and Featured Albums shelves.
- The smart-playlist banner wraps smoothly in both directions, with one dot per playlist, and appears
  at launch with the rest of Home.
- Station pages blend their artwork and add Shuffle; starting a station no longer shows a toast.

**Library**
- Pull-down search in every library section, ranked by exact name, prefix and typo correction.
- Sorting, plus genre and mood filters in Songs, with new moods: Feel-good, Party, Love songs,
  Sad & reflective, Dark, Focus and Throwback.

**Tags and artwork**
- Fix tags fills each missing field from the best verified match across MusicBrainz, iTunes and
  Discogs, writes all selected fields together, and can repair a single album, artist, playlist or
  song in place. Progress survives relaunches without resetting reviewed tracks.
- Edition-aware cover matching, Wikimedia Commons artist photos with credits, and verified caching.
- Upgrade covers shows your current image beside each match and can apply every verified upgrade at
  once. In the animated build, albums with a downloaded animated cover are marked and can be skipped.

**Playback**
- Ogg Vorbis and Opus decode on the device.
- Transitions can be edited between any two songs.
- Audio output starts only when you play something.

**App**
- erhu checks GitHub Releases when it opens and offers newer builds of your variant, with release
  notes, Skip for now, and Do not show this version again.
- VYBE's built-in model now gives the same results on the CPU and GPU.
- A Support erhu link to Ko-fi in **Settings → About Erhu**, for anyone who wants to help get erhu onto
  the App Store.

This is a beta. Report reproducible issues through the repository's issue forms and use
[the security policy](https://github.com/MeezaUni/Erhu-iOS/security/policy) for vulnerabilities.
The source archives, GPLv3 text, third-party notices, and checksums accompany the IPAs.
