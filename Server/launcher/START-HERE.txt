Erhu Server
===========

Stream or download your own music in Erhu on your iPhone, from this computer.

1. Open "ErhuServer" (Windows: ErhuServer.exe. Mac: the Erhu Server app).
   - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - On a Mac, the first time: right-click the app and choose Open. If macOS still refuses, open
     System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
     If it then says the app is damaged, run this once in Terminal, then open it again:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/Erhu Server.app"
   These warnings appear because this test build isn't code-signed yet.
2. A page opens in your browser. Choose your music folder, then create a username and password.
   If Windows asks whether to allow "navidrome" on the network, choose Allow (private networks).
3. On your iPhone (same Wi-Fi), point the Camera at the QR code on the page and tap
   "Open in Erhu". Type your password and tap Connect.

Erhu Server keeps running in the tray (Windows, near the clock) or the menu bar (Mac). Click its
icon to open the page again or to quit. Keep the computer on and awake while you listen.

Keep all the files in this folder together: Erhu Server needs the "navidrome" file next to it.

Erhu Server runs Navidrome (https://www.navidrome.org), open-source software under the GPL-3.0.
