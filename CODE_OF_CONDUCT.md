# Code of conduct

Erhu welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
This policy covers project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss the work rather than attacking the person.
- Give clear, constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs, and other people's time.
- Help keep discussion relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and spam are
not acceptable. Do not publish somebody else's private information without permission.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's report-content controls on the relevant comment or thread. Where available,
report to repository maintainers; private abuse can also be reported through
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not repeat private information or abusive material in a public report.

Maintainers may ask for changes, remove content, close threads, restrict participation, or block
accounts, depending on severity and repeated behavior. Moderation applies to maintainers too.
Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
